The problem I solve

Most Agile transformations stall after the mechanics go in.

Standups, boards, and PI events get installed — but the outcomes never arrive. Predictability stays low, scope creeps in mid-sprint without trade-off conversations, leaders lose visibility, and teams comply with the ceremonies without owning their delivery. I close that gap, and I put a number on the improvement.

  • Predictability stuck at 60–70%, with chronic carry-over and missed commitments.
  • A new or wobbling Release Train that needs PI Planning stood up and cadence enforced.
  • No delivery visibility — manual reporting, no metrics, no early warning for leaders.
  • Compliant but disengaged teams doing Agile without being Agile.
How I coach

I don't coach by telling.

Real coaching is mostly listening — observing before I advise, collaborating so the answer belongs to the team, not to me. A team that's told what to do depends on the coach; a team that discovers it owns the work.

You don't hand a team ownership. You birth it — by listening, observing, and helping them find their own answer.

About Wendell

Agile transformation leader, coach, and motivator.

I've known my purpose since I was thirteen: to help.

That's not a line from a leadership course — it's just true. Helping people is, honestly, my dopamine; it's where my joy comes from. Most people draw a line between their work happiness and their personal happiness — I never learned how. For me the joy is the same whatever the source: help a neighbor, help my family, help a team find its footing, and it all lands in the same place.

That's why servant leadership isn't a style I put on for work. It's who I am — and it's the reason it works. When I coach a team I'm not managing resources or running a playbook; I'm doing the thing I've wanted to do my whole life: helping people become the best version of themselves, together. The metrics follow — but the reason I show up is the people.

For more than 20 years I've partnered with executives and teams to turn modernization initiatives into real business outcomes. I help organizations decide where to begin, streamline operations, implement responsible AI governance, optimize processes, upskill their people, and build capabilities that last well beyond the engagement.

My foundation is engineering: a BS in Electronic Engineering (DeVry Institute, 1984), followed by years as a field engineer and in quality and process improvement before I ever ran a standup. That's why release readiness, test strategy, and non-functional requirements are conversations I have rather than escalate.

I hold CSM and CSP certifications, Leading SAFe training, PMP training, Coaching Skills for Leaders & Managers, Jira Align Program Essentials, Jira Fundamentals, Kanban — Lean Scheduling & JIT, and PMI Gen AI credentials — and I've coached teams through SAFe, Scrum, and Kanban at enterprise scale. Based in Keller, TX — available for on-site, hybrid, or remote engagements.
