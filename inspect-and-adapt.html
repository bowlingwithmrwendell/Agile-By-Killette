Inspect & Adapt: Listen First, Lead Second

Inspect & Adapt is a core Agile principle. It runs through planning and execution — and it matters most on the first day with a new team, when the most useful thing a servant leader can do is listen.
Lead. Listen. Elevate.

This is how I work, not a concept. Every team I join gets the same opening move: I listen before I change anything. The team will tell you how you can help — if you give them the room to.

Your first duty is to listen

A new Scrum Master or coach is tempted to walk in as the change agent — to arrive with the fix already in hand. I don’t. Trust starts in the first few calls and keeps building through small, deliberate moves — like any new relationship — and it starts with hearing how the team actually works before suggesting how it should. The moves I use are in Trust & Protection →

“Great teams aren’t built by change. They’re built by listening.”
Listen first. Lead second.

The Inspect & Adapt mindset

  1. Understandthe team’s needs, desires, and culture — before touching the process.
  2. Find the gapsand the opportunities for improvement the team already senses but hasn’t named.
  3. Co-create the pathforward with the team, not for the team. Ownership starts here.
  4. Make small, meaningful changesand keep iterating. Small moves the team can see working beat a big-bang rollout.
  5. Build trustengagement, and a stronger, higher-performing team.

The cycle never stops

PLAN→EXECUTE→INSPECT→ADAPT→RE-PLAN

Same team. New chapter.

When you start with listening, you learn more than you expect. You gain trust, uncover what the team really needs, and lay the foundation for a strong team agreement.
