The principles behind every agent

Bounded authority

The model can read and propose. It gets no tool that writes records or sends anything on its own.

Deterministic math

Every figure comes from tested code. The model is never asked to calculate a number.

Humans approve

Outbound actions, deployments and every AI answer in the PMO and Data Engineering agents wait for a named person to decide.
