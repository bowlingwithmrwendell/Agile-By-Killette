How I work — named methods

  • Ishikawa / fishbone analysis. Analytical problem-solving to decompose ambiguous problems into causes you can actually act on.
  • Open–Narrow–Close decisioning. How I facilitate cross-team working sessions — open the space, narrow the options, close on a commitment. It is how a room of competing priorities leaves with one decision.
  • Green / Yellow / Red health reporting. Periodic status against defined thresholds, each with a pre-planned mitigation. Leaders see the problem and the response in the same view.
  • Value stream mapping with flow metrics. Cycle time, WIP, throughput, and predictability — measured, not asserted. See the full approach →
  • Shu-Ha-Ri coaching to independence. Three scrum teams taken to “Ha” maturity — running their own practice without me.
